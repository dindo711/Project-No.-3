MPDO FUNCTIONS

LOCAL GOVERNMENT CODE

Pursuant to Section 476 of Republic Act No. 7160, otherwise known as the Local Government Code of 1991, the Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO) serves as the primary planning and development arm of the Local Government Unit. It is mandated to formulate integrated development plans, policies, programs, and projects; coordinate planning activities among various sectors; monitor and evaluate development initiatives; and promote participatory and sustainable local governance.

Read More

CORE FUNCTIONS

The MPDO is responsible for the preparation and updating of the Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP), Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP), Annual Investment Program (AIP), and other local development plans. It also undertakes project monitoring and evaluation, GIS mapping services, investment programming, data management, policy coordination, and technical planning support to ensure sustainable and inclusive development in the Municipality of Isulan.

Acts on applications for variances, exceptions, and appeals on zoning decisions to ensure fair and equitable implementation of the Municipal Zoning Ordinance.
